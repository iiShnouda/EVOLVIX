# API checks

End-to-end checks that call a running SKB server. They create their own data (names carry a
random tag), so they run on a fresh database or on a copy of a real one. Never point them at the
live company server.

| Script | What it checks |
|---|---|
| `catalog.test.mjs` | Units, price levels, prices and their permissions, size × color models, invoices and returns in cartons, reports by model, and a concurrency race (DOMAIN.md step 3). |
| `charges.test.mjs` | Invoice charges (discount chain, VAT, additions), bonus lines, sales reps, their permissions, exact returns, supplier discounts in the average cost. |
| `parties.test.mjs` | A factory is a supplier (old FACTORY input and filter still work), party details (other phones, owner / person in charge, factory and shipping addresses, notes), search by phone and owner, delivered sales starting at the shipping address; items' main supplier and the supplier's code (search by supplier, supplier code and price, with supplier prices hidden from sales staff), the first supplier bought from becoming the main one, and a model's variants following its supplier. |
| `costs.test.mjs` | Costs and profit only for the owner and whoever gets `costs:view`: sales documents, items (average cost; supplier prices only with purchases), stock list, item card, valuation, warehouses, profitability, performance, dead stock and the ledger (trial balance / income statement / balance sheet refused, stock and cost-of-sales accounts left out) — then granted to one person. A discount on one line needs `sales:discount` (not change-price), can't exceed the line or go below cost. |
| `departments.test.mjs` | Sales departments (أقسام): departments and a category's department; a sale belongs to one department and holds only its items (or items of no department); a person limited to departments sells, searches, lists and returns only in theirs (one = picked for them, several = must choose); closed departments; performance by department. Discount limits: the company's and a person's own (line discount plus its share of the invoice discount), none for the owner, and nobody gives a bigger limit than their own. |
| `limits.test.mjs` | Purchase limits and the accountant's checkout (owner, 2026-10-02): over the limit everyone's sale waits; a receipt that brings the customer within accepts what waits (oldest first, only what fits) and says so; a change over the limit is saved and waits (accepted or turned down = cancelled); returns only after Settings → hours; a return without the invoice counts against the customer's invoice line; the walk-in cash customer (code 0) can't be closed or changed; a discount on one invoice and one shared over several (percent / amount, all or nothing, one customer); a customer's invoices of the day with the previous balance; selling at the supply price needs `costs:view`. |
| `orders.test.mjs` | Pending invoices (فواتير معلّقة): a sales person saves one (priced and checked like an invoice, paid-now dropped, no cost shown); its goods are held against other pending and direct sales, nothing else moves; only an approver approves: changed discounts are checked against the approver's own limit, kept ones are not; payment taken, the rest on the account, rep, department and notes kept, approved once; cancelling (maker or approver) frees the goods; the customer's account per department with every invoice's discount, paid, left and notes, pending total, no cost; a cheque settling an invoice; the party statement like the old كشف حساب (book = department or cash box, document number, notes, items, branch filter); changing a pending invoice (maker or approver, same number, rechecked); customers' credit limits (who may set them, sales and approvals over them refused unless allowed); how long pending invoices hold their goods (with `PGURL`); notifications to approvers and back to the maker, rejecting with a reason; every sale's rep defaulting to its maker; sellers writing returns (back into the same warehouse) and the shipping we pay on returns and purchases as an expense; sales-list date and product category filters. |
| `codes.test.mjs` | Codes (كود) for customers, suppliers and staff (numbered in order, found by code or by searching the number, kept on rename, on the reps list and on invoices); a sales invoice posts at once and whoever takes payments (`sales:invoice_alerts`, in its department) is told the customer, department and amount — not its maker, not someone in another department; adding to a price on one invoice (`sales:raise_price`) but not lowering it; each person's work kept on the server while the app updates (saved, replaced, taken back once, only by its owner, size limits); the invoice list like the old screen (code, rep, total, net, paid, left on each row; on-account / paid filters; totals of everything the filter finds, whatever the page); the detailed item search (code / name contain, price range, stock in / out / at reorder level, no supplier); a sale's due date (today or later, kept on it); each person's screen preferences (saved, theirs only, forgotten when emptied); every journal entry's own running number on the statement. |
| `invoices.test.mjs` | Changing a posted sales invoice (`sales:edit`: same id and number; stock, the customer's balance, money paid now and the ledger follow each version) and cancelling it (`sales:cancel`: everything back, listed as cancelled, not twice, not once goods were returned or a receipt was taken against it); the list's payment state (credit, partly paid, paid, cancelled) and its branch / warehouse filters; pricing at the selling price, the customer's last price or cost (`sales:price_basis`; cost only shown to `costs:view`); the shipping company and address from the customer's file (`sales:change_shipping` to change them); cash customers pay in full (`parties:cash_customer` to mark one); the department rule (Settings); the serial number's prefix, start and digits (Settings), never going back. |
| `branding.test.mjs` | The company's own name and logo beside the Evolvix mark: readable before sign-in; only `settings:manage` uploads or removes the logo; the picture must really be a PNG, JPEG or WebP (an SVG/script or bad base64 is refused, so is one over 512 KB); served back unchanged with `nosniff` and a sandbox CSP; the version moves on each change; audited. |
| `delivery.test.mjs` | Parties add/edit/close, the delivery method on a sale, own deliveries, parcels with cash on delivery, refused parcels (returns), carrier settlements, permissions, branch scoping and races (DOMAIN.md step 4b). |
| `ledger.test.mjs` | Chart of accounts, the entry every document posts (line by line), account and party statements, trial balance, manual entries and reversal, the period lock and permissions (DOMAIN.md step 6). |
| `statements.test.mjs` | Income statement and balance sheet from the ledger (and how a sale, purchase and expense move them), monthly sales, top customers / suppliers, item movement and dead stock, permissions (DOMAIN.md step 8). |
| `notes.test.mjs` | Cheques & promissory notes: receive / issue with invoices, deposit portfolios, collect, pay, bounce, endorse, cancel — each step's balances, money and journal entry — permissions, scoping and a race (DOMAIN.md step 7). |
| `money.test.mjs` | Money accounts, receipts and payments with allocation, expenses, transfers, cancelling, paid-now / refunds / settlements through accounts, the no-negative rule, permissions, branch scoping and races (DOMAIN.md step 5). |
| `staff.test.mjs` | Staff & permissions: people, roles, per-person grants/revokes, and the safety rules (no self-escalation, owners protected, no self-lockout). |
| `admin.test.mjs` | Company profile, the activity log, aging of debts (every bucket with `PGURL`), the year-end close (entry, statements, lock, race) and permissions. **Run it last:** a closed year stays closed, so its close checks run once per database. |
| `payroll.test.mjs` | Payroll & attendance: shifts, employees, attendance (typed and from a fingerprint export) with late / overtime, bonuses, deductions, advances, the month's payroll to the piastre, its journal entry, recovery, locks after posting, paying, a race and permissions. Runs in its own new branch. |
| `access.test.mjs` | Access per person: branches (by role, every one, theirs plus some), the working branch (X-Branch), only some warehouses (selling, default store, stock, item card, valuation) and money accounts (paid-now, receipts, cancelling, statements), who may give what, audit. Runs in two new branches with its own people. |
| `time.test.mjs` | Company days (Settings → time zone): documents just before / after a Cairo midnight land on the right day, month, aging bucket, statement, daily cash, item card and valuation, across the daylight-saving jump and fall back; then the same under Dubai and UTC, with SQL grouping agreeing with single-day totals. Needs `PGURL` for most checks. |
| `pricing.test.mjs` | Price lists and quantity discounts: a party's list, units from the base, whole models, dates, closing, offers for everyone, quantity steps (per item, per model, own steps first, free goods), what a sales person may charge, a supplier's list, validation, deleting, audit. |
| `quotes.test.mjs` | Price quotations: priced like invoices but moving nothing, turned into invoices (prices held while valid, stock checked, once even in a race), expiry (with `PGURL`), cancelling, permissions, audit; sales performance by person and branch. |
| `system.test.mjs` | Who's online over the real SignalR hub (sign-in required, several windows, screen names cleaned, only permitted people told) and backups: permissions, the encrypted download, automatic backups in the server folder (with `Backup__Folder`, `Backup__Keep=2`), audit. |
| `restore-check.mjs` | After `SKB.Api backup` + `SKB.Api restore` into a new database: every table's rows match the original (`PGURL` vs `PGURL_RESTORED`). |
| `invariants.mjs` | Database invariants: stock = Σ balances = Σ movements, one base unit per item, line quantities and totals, returns never exceeding their line. Needs `npm i pg`. |

## Running

```bash
# 1. a server on a throwaway database (Development settings, high rate limits)
ASPNETCORE_ENVIRONMENT=Development ConnectionStrings__Default="Host=localhost;Port=5432;Database=skb_check;Username=skb;Password=..." \
  RateLimit__UserPerMinute=100000 ASPNETCORE_URLS=http://localhost:5090 dotnet run --project apps/api/src/SKB.Api

# 2. the checks (Node 22)
BASE=http://localhost:5090/api node scripts/checks/catalog.test.mjs
PGURL=postgres://skb:...@localhost:5432/skb_check node scripts/checks/invariants.mjs

```

The checks run with Settings → "every sale names its department" off (`PUT /settings {requireDepartment:false}`) and returns allowed at once (`returnAfterHours: 0`); `limits.test.mjs` sets both at its start, so run it first. `invoices.test.mjs` turns the department rule on and off itself.

The seeded demo users are used: `owner@skb.local` (Owner), `mona@skb.local` (Accountant),
`karim@skb.local` (Sales) and `sara@skb.local` (Inventory).
